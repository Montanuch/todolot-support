What TodoLot does

TodoLot turns your voice notes into scheduled tasks: you dictate, an AI service splits the dictation into tasks, times, and advance reminders, and each task becomes a system alarm that speaks the task out loud when it rings. Some features — natural alarm voices and unlimited AI dictation — require a paid subscription (TodoLot Pro). Without one, AI dictation is limited to a small number of parses per day, while manually created tasks are always free and unlimited. We may change what is included in the free tier and in Pro over time.

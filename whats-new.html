What’s new in TodoLot

Release notes for the iPhone app. Current version: 1.01.

1.01

October 2026

  • Calendar. Pinch the list or tap the calendar button to see the whole month, with an agenda for any day and a tray for tasks without a date. Drag a task to another day to move it; the move can be undone.
  • What happens after an alarm. Stopping an alarm no longer marks the task done. A “Just rang” card lets you choose: done, ring again tonight, or tomorrow. Tasks you did not resolve collect in a Missed section.
  • Dictation tips. A “?” chip on the recording screen opens “What you can say”, with examples you can tap to try. A short “what + when” sample appears above the dictate button until you have used the pattern.
  • Full-width buttons on the dictation screens.
  • Fixes. Checking a task off now cancels its live alarm, including while it is snoozed. Long dictations no longer cut off around the one-minute mark. Snoozing and then marking done behaves consistently.

1.0

August 15, 2026

  • First release. Dictate a brain-dump; AI splits it into tasks with times; each task becomes a real iOS alarm that says the task out loud.
  • Natural voices (Pro), morning briefing, repeating tasks, advance reminders (“a day before”), quiet notification-only tasks, someday list, Siri shortcut.
  • Free tier: 3 voice captures a day with the system voice, unlimited manual tasks.

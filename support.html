TodoLot Support

Speak your tasks — hear them right on time.

TodoLot is a voice-to-alarm to-do app for iOS made by an independent developer. Questions, bug reports, feature ideas, or help with a subscription — we read every message.

Contact

Email us at vibeprogerai@gmail.com. Please mention your iOS version and, if relevant, what you dictated and what you expected to happen — it helps us fix things faster. You can also reach us from the app: Settings → Feedback.

Common questions

  • An alarm didn’t speak or didn’t ring. Check that TodoLot is allowed to schedule alarms (iOS Settings → TodoLot) and that the device was on and charged at the alarm time. See also the alarm notes in the Terms of Use.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription. iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore a purchase. In the app, open the paywall or Settings → Subscription and tap Restore.
  • Delete my data. See Privacy Policy → Your rights and choices, or just email us.

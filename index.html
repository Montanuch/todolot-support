TodoLot Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

TodoLot is a voice-to-alarm to-do app for iOS made by an independent developer. Your tasks live on your device. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and your data is never sold. This page describes exactly what leaves your device, where it goes, and why.

What the app processes, and where

Microphone and speech recognition

When you dictate tasks, your speech is transcribed by Apple’s built-in iOS speech recognition, with your permission. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on the device or on Apple’s servers — see Apple’s Privacy Policy. Your audio never reaches our servers or any other third party.

Turning dictation into tasks

The text of your dictation (never the audio) is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service — Anthropic or OpenAI — which splits it into individual tasks and times. Along with the text, the request includes your dictation language, timezone, the current time, and the names of your task groups (so the AI can sort tasks into them). Requests may be routed through our lightweight relay server (hosted on Cloudflare), which keeps API credentials off your device and enforces a daily rate limit using a random device identifier — a UUID generated on your device that is not linked to your name, email, or any account; its daily counters expire and are deleted automatically within 48 hours. We do not store or log your dictation text.

Natural alarm voice

If the natural alarm voice is enabled, the text of an alarm phrase (and of the morning briefing) is sent over an encrypted connection to ElevenLabs, which generates the audio. The generated audio is cached on your device so the same phrase is not sent twice. If the natural voice is turned off, or the network is unavailable, the voice is generated entirely on your device using Apple’s built-in text-to-speech.

Anonymous identifier, usage analytics, and crash reports

On first launch the app creates an anonymous user ID via Google Firebase Authentication. It is a random identifier — no name, email, or password is ever asked for or collected. The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (events like app opens and screen views, plus device model, OS version, and coarse region — including steps of the setup and purchase flow, never the content of your tasks) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. This data is linked to the anonymous ID, not to your identity, and is used only to improve the app.

Purchases

Billing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details. To reliably unlock your subscription across reinstalls, the app records subscription status (which plan is active and its renewal state, nothing more) with RevenueCat, keyed to the same anonymous ID.

Tasks, alarms, and settings

Your tasks, alarm times, checklists, groups, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you use iCloud or device backups, this local data may be included in your backups, which are managed by Apple.

What we don’t do

  • No sign-up: we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • Your dictation audio never leaves Apple’s speech recognition.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app deletes your tasks, settings, and generated audio from your device. We keep no copy of your dictations. Server-side, the anonymous daily rate counter deletes itself within 48 hours; analytics events, crash reports, and subscription status are retained by Firebase and RevenueCat under their policies, keyed only to the anonymous ID. To have that data deleted, email us the request — we will remove it.

Children

TodoLot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

TodoLot Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

TodoLot is a voice-to-alarm to-do app for iOS made by an independent developer. Your tasks live on your device. There is no account to create, no advertising, and your personal data is never sold. This page describes exactly what leaves your device, where it goes, and why — in plain language.

At a glance

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone, or a password.
  • Your tasks stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Only two things leave the app on purpose: the text of a dictation (to turn it into tasks) and, if enabled, the text of an alarm phrase (to generate a natural voice). Both go over encrypted connections.
  • Everything else is anonymous, aggregate usage analytics and crash reports, tied to a random ID — not to you.

What the app processes, and where

Microphone and speech recognition

When you dictate tasks, your speech is transcribed by Apple’s built-in iOS speech recognition, with your permission. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on the device or on Apple’s servers — see Apple’s Privacy Policy. Your audio never reaches our servers or any other third party.

Turning dictation into tasks

The text of your dictation (never the audio) is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service — Anthropic or OpenAI — which splits it into individual tasks, times, and any advance reminders. Along with the text, the request includes your dictation language, timezone, the current time, and the names of your task groups (so the AI can sort tasks into them). Requests may be routed through our lightweight relay server (hosted on Cloudflare), which keeps API credentials off your device and enforces a daily rate limit using a random device identifier — a UUID generated on your device that is not linked to your name, email, or any account; its daily counters expire and are deleted automatically within 48 hours. We do not store or log your dictation text, and we instruct these providers not to use it to train their models.

Natural alarm voice

If the natural alarm voice is enabled, the text of an alarm phrase (and of the morning briefing) is sent over an encrypted connection to ElevenLabs, which generates the audio. The generated audio is cached on your device so the same phrase is not sent twice. If the natural voice is turned off, or the network is unavailable, the voice is generated entirely on your device using Apple’s built-in text-to-speech.

Anonymous identifier, usage analytics, and crash reports

On first launch the app creates an anonymous user ID via Google Firebase Authentication. It is a random identifier — no name, email, or password is ever asked for or collected. Using this anonymous ID, the app collects:

  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) to understand how the app is used and how much it costs us to run — for example app opens and screen views; the steps you take through setup and purchase; permission choices (granted or denied); and simple counts such as how many tasks a dictation produced and how many characters of speech were generated. These are counts and event names only — never the content, wording, or subject of your tasks or dictations.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Standard technical context that these Google SDKs collect automatically, such as device model, operating-system version, and a coarse region derived from your IP address.

This data is linked to the anonymous ID, not to your identity, and is used only to operate and improve the app.

Purchases

Billing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details. To reliably unlock your subscription across reinstalls, the app records subscription status (which plan is active and its renewal state, nothing more) with RevenueCat, keyed to the same anonymous ID.

Tasks, alarms, and settings

Your tasks, alarm times, checklists, groups, reminders, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you use iCloud or device backups, this local data may be included in your backups, which are managed by Apple.

What we don’t do

  • No sign-up: we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No advertising and no ad networks; we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • Your dictation audio never leaves Apple’s speech recognition.
  • We never send the content of your tasks to analytics or crash tools.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to object to certain processing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we do not hold your name or email, we identify your records by the anonymous ID or device UUID associated with your app. You can:

  • Stop analytics collection by turning off analytics tracking in your iOS settings, or remove the anonymous data footprint by deleting the app.
  • Delete your data — email us and we will delete the analytics, crash, and subscription records associated with your anonymous ID.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app deletes your tasks, settings, and generated audio from your device. We keep no copy of your dictations. Server-side, the anonymous daily rate counter deletes itself within 48 hours; analytics events, crash reports, and subscription status are retained by Firebase and RevenueCat under their policies, keyed only to the anonymous ID. To have that data deleted, email us the request — we will remove it.

Children

TodoLot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

How it works

  1. Brain-dump by voice. Hold the button and say everything at once: “call the bank at 9, pick up the parcel after work, gym every Monday”.
  2. AI splits it into tasks. Each task gets its own time, group, repeat rule or advance reminder. You review and confirm before anything is scheduled.
  3. Your phone rings and tells you what to do. Each task is a real system alarm that speaks the task itself. No unlocking, no reading, no swiping it away.

What’s inside

  • Natural voices (Pro): choose who reminds you, from gentle Sarah to no-nonsense Harry. Voices are tuned for English plus Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi and Arabic.
  • Morning briefing: your day, spoken to you when you wake up.
  • Repeating tasks: “gym every Monday and Thursday”, said once, scheduled forever.
  • Advance reminders: “dentist on the 12th, remind me a day before”.
  • Quiet tasks: things that only need a gentle notification, not an alarm.
  • Someday list: ideas without a date, kept until you give them one.
  • Month calendar: see the whole month, drag a task to another day.
  • Your time zone, your words: “at 9” and “on Friday” are understood in your local time, in any of the languages you dictate in.
  • Siri: add a task by voice from anywhere.
  • Wake-up sound before the voice: birds, a chime, waves, or your own (Pro).
  • Privacy by design: no account, tasks stay on your device, no ads.

Frequently asked

Does TodoLot ring on silent mode or in Focus?

Yes. Alarms are real iOS system alarms, so they ring at full volume on silent and in Focus modes. Quiet tasks, which you mark on purpose, arrive as normal notifications instead.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later. On iPad the app runs as an iPhone-designed app.

Which languages can I dictate in?

Any language that iOS speech recognition supports on your device. You pick the dictation language on the recording screen. Natural voices are tuned for English and eleven other languages listed above.

Does it work offline?

Tasks you add by hand and all scheduled alarms work without a connection. Turning a dictation into tasks and generating a natural voice need the internet; if the network is down, the alarm falls back to the built-in system voice so it always rings.

Is my data private?

Your tasks live only on your device. There is no account and no advertising. Only the text of a dictation and, with natural voices on, the text of an alarm phrase leave the app. Full details: Privacy Policy.

Is TodoLot made for ADHD?

It is built for forgetful brains in general: tasks that evaporate the moment you think “I’ll remember that”, reminders that pile up ignored. Many people with ADHD find a loud, talking alarm easier to act on than a silent banner. TodoLot is a convenience tool, not a medical device.
